Chapter One

Season I · An Origin Story

They said the old world had run its course — that the furnaces had cooled and the contracts had gone unsigned. Then a name appeared on the ledgers, written in a hand nobody recognised: Jondique. And with it, an offer no honest soul could refuse.

The first season opens on a city being built from the ground up — cobblestone giving way to cast-iron, villages keeping their secrets behind frosted glass. Coal-smoke drifts across the harbour and the trains have only just begun to run on time.

You are not the hero of this story. You are the one who arrived with a suitcase, a pickaxe, and a quiet talent for being in the right alley at the wrong hour. Build accordingly.
